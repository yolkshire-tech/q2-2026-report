## 1. Executive Summary

Both independent analyses converge on the same core insight: **pair high-volume mains with high-margin beverages**. 

To maximize reach, we have classified all combos into **High Protein** (meat, eggs, or substantial protein) and **Value** (vegetarian/light) categories. Per your directive, **all Value combos have been price-capped at ₹299** to ensure they serve as true affordability anchors without cannibalizing premium offerings.

The ultimate winner remains **The Stroganoff Refresher** (Chicken Stroganoff + Mint Lemonade) – an 80% margin powerhouse. Top "Value" winners are **The Garden Delight** (Veg Alfredo + Mint Mojito) and **The Pasta Classic** (Veg Alfredo + Garlic Bread + Iced Tea) – both fixed at **₹299** for maximum accessibility.

**Impact**: ₹1.2L – ₹3.0L additional profit/quarter at conservative adoption.

**Correction Note**: All omelettes at Yolkshire come standard with toast. Therefore, **The Mumbai Morning** (Masala Omelette + Mint Lemonade) is a complete breakfast meal as-is – no additional carb component needed.
