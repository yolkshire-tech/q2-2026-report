# Yolkshire Q2 2026 Combo Strategy & Launch Recommendations

**Analysis period:** April–June 2026 (Q2)  
**Prepared:** July 2026  
**Purpose:** A stakeholder-ready consolidation of the Q2 market-basket analyses, focused on the recommended combo menu and launch priorities.

---

## Executive TL;DR

Yolkshire’s clearest route to higher order value is to convert main-only and light-basket occasions into complete meals with high-margin drinks, sides, or breakfast add-ons. The strongest offers use a familiar, high-volume main—particularly Chicken Stroganoff or Roast Chicken—and pair it with a margin-accretive beverage.

Launch a small core menu first: two hero chicken meals, two breakfast meals, and two café/delivery offers. Value offers are capped at ₹300, preferably ₹250; High Protein meals retain premium pricing when their portion and contribution support it.

> **Data note:** The source reports disagree on headline sales and AOV baselines. Use this report to prioritise the pilot menu; reconcile reporting definitions before approving final revenue targets.

---

## Key Insights

- High-margin beverages are under-attached to mains, especially at breakfast and on delivery orders.
- Chicken Stroganoff and Roast Chicken are the strongest core anchors for a new combo programme.
- Breakfast needs complete meal architecture: omelettes already include toast; pair them with a suitable beverage and, where relevant, a side.
- Mint Lemonade, coffee, iced tea, and selected juices are attractive attach items because they have strong margins and improve meal completeness.
- Customer logic matters: replace dessert pairings that clash with savoury eggs or protein meals with a beverage that fits the occasion.

## Recommended Pilot: First Four Weeks

Launch the following six offers first:

1. The Stroganoff Signature Meal
2. Roast & Refresh
3. The Executive English Brunch
4. The Grilled Chicken Feast
5. The Chicken Café Classic
6. Peri-Peri Power Breakfast

Feature them in a single aggregator section—**Yolkshire Signature Meals & Brews**—and use one simple staff prompt for each anchor item. For example: “Would you like to make that a Stroganoff Signature Meal with Garlic Bread and Lemon Iced Tea?”

Measure weekly combo adoption, beverage attachment, incremental AOV versus comparable non-combo orders, contribution profit, and repeat purchase. Add the remaining ranked offers only after the first-wave results are clear.

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Why It Matters | Initial Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Combo adoption rate | Shows whether customers accept the new offer architecture. | 10–15% of eligible orders | Weekly POS and aggregator report |
| Beverage attachment rate | Tests the core opportunity identified in both analyses. | 40%+ of relevant main orders | Item pairing / basket analysis |
| Incremental AOV | Confirms combos expand baskets rather than only discount them. | ₹20+ versus comparable non-combo orders | Weekly cohort comparison |
| Contribution profit per combo | Protects profitability when offer prices are introduced or changed. | At or above approved food-cost floor | Recipe costing and POS sales data |
| Repeat purchase | Identifies sustainable meal occasions rather than one-off promotion purchases. | Track by combo and session | Monthly customer/order analysis |
| Anchor-item volume | Detects cannibalisation of the core main item. | Non-declining total anchor demand | Compare standalone-equivalent volume weekly |

---

## Final Recommendation

Use a compact, named menu—not a long list of discounts. Make the two hero chicken meals and the two breakfast offers highly visible, give Value customers clear ₹250–₹300 choices, and retain premium pricing only where protein, portion, and contribution justify it.
