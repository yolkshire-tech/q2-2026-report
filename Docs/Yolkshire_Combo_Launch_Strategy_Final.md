# Yolkshire Combo Launch Strategy — Final Report

**Basis:** Confirmed Q2 2026 sales data (revenue, unit volume) + actual menu food-cost data, with near-identical item names merged (e.g., three case-variant spellings of "Grilled Chicken Breast" consolidated into one true figure). Recipe costs carry an assumed margin of error of roughly ±5 percentage points — treat margin figures as directional, not exact to the rupee.

**Strategy:** Pair proven high-volume "star" dishes with underselling high-margin items ("puzzles" — beverages and sides with 78–92% margin that are rarely attached to a main). Every combo below is buildable and deliverable on **both dine-in and online/aggregator channels** with no menu-availability gaps.

## 3. Success Metrics — KPIs & Targets

| Metric | Target | Measurement | Why It Matters |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Combo adoption rate | 12% by Week 4, 18%+ by Week 8 | Weekly POS + aggregator report | Core signal customers accept the new architecture |
| AOV gain per combo order | ≥15% vs. standalone anchor price | Per-order comparison, not restaurant-wide average | The real per-transaction upsell value |

| Blended combo margin | ≥70% dine-in / ≥45% post-commission delivery | Recipe costing + POS | Protects profitability as prices move |
| Anchor cannibalization | Standalone anchor volume non-declining (≤5% dip tolerated) | Weekly standalone-vs-combo volume split | Confirms combos add, not just relabel, sales |
| Shakshuka volume lift | 2x baseline (733 → 1,400+/quarter) | Weekly sales report | Tests whether the promo combo actually moves the underselling dish |
| Channel split performance | Track dine-in vs. delivery adoption separately | Channel mix report | Delivery and dine-in behave differently — don't average them away |
| Repeat purchase rate | Combo buyers show higher repeat rate than non-combo buyers | Monthly cohort analysis | Identifies real habit formation vs. one-off promo pull |
| Staff upsell conversion (dine-in only) | 50%+ of eligible tables offered the upsell | Mystery shopping / POS tagging | Combos don't sell themselves — this is the execution layer |

## 4. Phased Implementation Plan

### Phase 0 — Pilot (Weeks 1–2)
- **Combos:** Grilled Chicken Protein Bowl, Stroganoff Refresher, Executive English Brunch (High-Protein #1–3)
- **Scope:** 2–3 branches, dine-in only, no aggregator listing yet
- **Goal:** Validate POS combo-SKU setup, kitchen prep timing, and staff scripting before touching delivery channels
- **Exit criteria:** Adoption rate ≥8%, no material kitchen slowdown, margin tracking within 3pp of projection

### Phase 1 — Expansion (Weeks 3–4)
- **Combos:** Add Roast & Refresh, Shakshuka Sunrise (High-Protein #4–5) + Garden Delight, Masala Omelette Starter (Value tier)
- **Scope:** All branches, dine-in + Zomato/Swiggy aggregator listing goes live for Phase 0 combos
- **Ops focus:** Aggregator photography, "Yolkshire Signature Meals & Brews" featured section, delivery pricing set 5–8% above dine-in
- **Exit criteria:** Combo adoption ≥12%, beverage attachment ≥30%

### Phase 2 — Full Launch (Weeks 5–8)
- **Combos:** Remaining High-Protein tier (Alfredo Refresher, Chicken Café Classic, Peri-Peri Power Lunch, Peri-Peri Omelette Power) + Bhuna Roll Refresher (Value)
- **Scope:** All branches, all channels
- **Ops focus:** Full staff upsell-script rollout, loyalty-program tie-in for combo purchases, weekly KPI review cadence begins
- **Exit criteria:** Adoption ≥18%, all combos tracking ≥65% blended margin

### Phase 2.5 — Premium Soft-Launch (Week 7 onward, dine-in first)
- **Combos:** Stroganoff Deluxe, Peri-Peri Executive Platter, Roast Chicken Executive Feast
- **Scope:** Dine-in only initially — these are 3-item bundles at a materially higher price point and benefit from server framing ("chef's platter") rather than a menu-scroll impulse buy. Add to aggregators only after dine-in take-up is confirmed.
- **Ops focus:** Train staff to position these as the "step-up" option for guests already ordering a High-Protein combo, not as a replacement for it
- **Exit criteria:** Adoption ≥5% of eligible dine-in checks before adding to delivery channels

### Phase 3 — Optimize (Month 3+)
- A/B test beverage pairings on the two highest-reach combos (e.g., Stroganoff + Mint Lemonade vs. Stroganoff + Cold Coffee)
- Retire or reprice any combo below 60% margin or showing anchor cannibalization
- Evaluate a second wave built around other Small Plates protein add-ons, following the Grilled Chicken Protein Bowl model

## 5. Operational Guidelines

**Dine-in:**
- One-line staff script per anchor, e.g.: *"Would you like to make that a Stroganoff Refresher with Mint Lemonade for ₹379?"*
- Table-side QR menus should surface the combo as the default suggested option next to the standalone anchor, not buried in a separate "combos" page
- No aggregator commission to absorb — dine-in is where full 68–79% margins are actually realized

**Delivery/Aggregator:**
- List each combo as its own SKU on Zomato/Swiggy (not a manual add-on at checkout) — combo-as-SKU converts far better than a prompt
- Price 5–8% above dine-in to offset commission
- Packaging notes for combos that don't travel as cleanly:
  - **Executive English Brunch & Peri-Peri Omelette Power:** pack toast/hash browns separately from wet components to avoid sogginess
  - **Shakshuka Sunrise:** use a leak-proof container with the garlic bread packed separately
- Use "Chef's Special Combo" or "Signature Meal" badges consistently across both aggregators for brand recognition

## 7. Promotional Bundling Strategy

- **Featured placement:** All 11 combos live under one aggregator section — "Yolkshire Signature Meals & Brews" — rather than scattered across categories
- **Underselling-dish push:** Shakshuka Sunrise gets a limited-time "12% off first order" promo specifically to build trial for the highest-margin dish on the menu; pull the discount once volume target is hit, keep the combo
- **Loyalty tie-in:** Bonus points multiplier on combo purchases to nudge repeat behavior faster than price alone would
- **Combo-of-the-week spotlight:** Rotate visual/photography focus weekly across the 11 combos to keep the section from going stale, without touching price
- **No stacking:** Combos should not be eligible for additional platform-wide discount codes — protects the margin math above; make this an explicit aggregator-settings rule, not a manual check

## 8. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Anchor cannibalization — guests just swap standalone-item spend into the combo with no net gain | Track standalone-vs-combo volume weekly; if standalone volume drops faster than combo volume rises, reprice or pull |
| Aggregator commission compresses margin more than modeled | Delivery-channel pricing set 5–8% above dine-in from Day 1, not retrofitted later |
| Recipe cost data has an assumed margin of error (~±5pp per this analysis) | Re-verify actual food cost for the lowest-margin combo in each tier (Bhuna Roll Refresher, Peri-Peri Omelette Power, Peri-Peri Executive Platter) before their respective launch phases, since they have the least buffer |
| Menu/choice fatigue from launching too many combos at once | Phased rollout (Section 4) — never more than 3–6 live combos being actively pushed at a time |
| Staff inconsistency in upselling | Mystery-shop audit at Week 4; retrain branches below 40% conversion before Phase 2 |
| Wet/composite dishes underperforming on delivery due to poor packaging | Packaging notes in Section 5 applied before those specific combos go live on aggregators |
