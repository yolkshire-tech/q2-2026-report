Kothrud Model Replication & Footprint Expansion Playbook

🏆 The Kothrud Scaling Strategy

What the Kothrud model proves is: if you first achieve ₹20 Lakhs sales in a small, compact outlet (<500 sq ft) with tight rent and lean staffing, you establish brand traction with minimal risk. Once the ₹20L threshold is crossed, expanding the outlet seating footprint allows you to scale to ₹28L–35L+ sales with industry-leading EBITDA margins (25%+).

Key Franchisee Takeaway:
Do not start with an oversized 1,500 sq ft space. Replicate Kothrud: start compact, optimize table turn rate and delivery velocity to hit ₹20L, then expand into adjacent space.
