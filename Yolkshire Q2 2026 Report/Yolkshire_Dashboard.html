• Diversified channel presence (3 major channels)
• Coffee growing as strong breakfast companion (+700%)
• Bavdhan Month 1 AOV ₹593 — above chain avg
• Low discount rate (3.04%) — pricing power

⚡ Weaknesses

• Chicken Stroganoff = 9% of revenue — dependency
• June orders -1,021 vs May — demand softening
• 66 Dog items creating operational complexity
• 3–5 PM idle — 21 branch-hours/day wasted
• Wakad lowest AOV (₹497) — mix/pricing issue
• Constraint: scaling and cross-franchise consistency

🚀 Opportunities

• Weak day ROI: growing Monday by 20% vs Sunday by 5%
• Mon-Wed campaigns: Monday Breakfast Club, Student Tuesdays
• Coffee & comfort food pairings (72% basket confidence)
• Afternoon snack menu (3 idle hours/day × 7 branches)
• Systems rollout: replicate Kothrud's success playbook
• 8th branch using Bavdhan success model

⚠️ Threats

• Zomato/Swiggy ~25% commission = ₹27L/qtr drain
• Wadgaon Sheri competitive pressure — unidentified
• Monsoon reducing cafe foot traffic (Jun–Sep)
• Beverage competition: Starbucks, Third Wave Coffee
• Chicken cost inflation → impacts #1 revenue item
• Kothrud at 30% = chain-level concentration risk
