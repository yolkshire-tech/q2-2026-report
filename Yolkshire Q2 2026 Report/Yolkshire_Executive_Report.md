Yolkshire delivered **₹2.07 Crore in net revenue** across Q2 2026 — spread across 7 branches, 3 channels, and 91 days of operation. The most critical finding: **Chicken Stroganoff alone contributes 9.06% of all item revenue**, creating single-item dependency that represents both the brand's greatest strength and its greatest operational risk.

## 1.4 Top 10 Business Insights

1. **Coffee strengthens the egg-speciality positioning.** Espresso (+1,057%), Americano (+735%), Cappuccino (+672%) — coffee is growing rapidly. Rather than shifting away from being an egg-speciality cafe, coffee should be positioned as a companion to strengthen breakfast and afternoon traffic, enhancing comfort food sales without diluting the core brand.

2. **Weak weekdays represent the biggest growth opportunity.** Sunday generates ₹45.25L, but increasing Sunday revenue by 5% is extremely difficult as branches operate at peak capacity. Increasing Monday revenue (currently ₹21.96L) by 20% is much easier and offers a higher ROI. Weekday-specific campaigns (e.g., Monday Breakfast Club, Student Tuesdays, Wednesday Work from Yolkshire) will drive outsized returns.

3. **Kothrud is the crown jewel — but over-relied upon.** At 30% of total revenue, one branch's underperformance would materially impact the chain. Replication of Kothrud's success model is urgent.

4. **Bavdhan opened and instantly contributed ₹3.64L in a single month** at an AOV of ₹593 — higher than all older branches, including the flagship Kothrud. This new branch is outperforming expectations and needs aggressive investment.

5. **Zomato is more valuable than it appears.** At ₹485 AOV vs Swiggy's ₹441, Zomato customers spend 10% more. Yet Swiggy has 9,828 orders vs Zomato's 13,526. The platform mix should be actively managed.

6. **10 AM is the single most valuable hour in the business.** Every 10 AM operational decision — staffing, kitchen prep, table management — directly affects ₹20.86L of quarterly revenue. It must be fully optimized.

7. **Wadgaon Sheri is declining.** Revenue dropped Apr→May→Jun with a clear downward trajectory. Without intervention, this branch will continue losing ground.

8. **The top 58 items generate 80% of revenue.** The remaining 102 revenue items represent operational complexity for minimal return. Menu rationalization is a significant cost and efficiency opportunity.

9. **Meal + Beverage attach rate is the biggest upsell gap.** Analysis shows high confidence (0.72) that customers buying a main dish would also buy a beverage — but attach rates suggest this isn't happening consistently, representing daily revenue leakage.

10. **The 3–5 PM window is the most underutilized.** Only ₹13.54L across 91 days, at the lowest kitchen load of the day. A dedicated afternoon snack/coffee menu + digital push could unlock ₹15–20L of incremental revenue per quarter.

---

## 1.5 Scale Transition Point: Constrained by Systems, Not Menu
The data suggests Yolkshire has reached a critical scale transition point. At roughly ₹70 lakh per month in Q2 (and around ₹80 lakh now), the business is no longer constrained primarily by menu quality. Instead, growth is constrained by systems and replication:
*   **Consistent Execution**: Replicating the flagship operations across all franchises.
*   **Marketing Coordination**: Aligning cross-branch promotions and digital outreach.
*   **Delivery Platform Optimization**: Managing funnel metrics to convert online intent.
*   **Customer Retention**: Moving from transaction-only visits to loyalty-driven relationships.
*   **Standardized Training**: Building repeatable staff onboarding and upselling systems.
*   **Data-Driven Decisions**: Integrating sales and cost data for active management.

The next phase of growth won't come from inventing another great dish. It will come from making every branch operate more like the Kothrud branch while building repeatable marketing and operational systems across the network. This is the core strategic shift required for founders and prospective franchise investors.

## 1.6 Top 10 Business Recommendations
1. **Launch a Coffee & Breakfast Companion Program** — Leverage the growing coffee volume to increase average spend and strengthen breakfast and afternoon traffic, while reinforcing Yolkshire's core egg-speciality brand.
2. **Launch Weekday Activation Campaigns** — Implement targeted promotions on Mon-Wed (e.g., Monday Breakfast Club, Wednesday Work From Yolkshire, Student Tuesdays, Corporate Breakfasts) to capture low-hanging weekday revenue.
3. **Fix Wadgaon Sheri now** — Conduct a branch operations audit, identify local demand gaps, and run a 30-day revival campaign.
4. **Fast-track Bavdhan expansion** — With Month 1 AOV at ₹593, this branch has the highest revenue potential per order. Add staffing and expand dine-in capacity.
5. **Reduce menu to 80 items** — Remove or rotate the 102 low-performing items. Simplified menus improve kitchen speed, reduce food waste, and increase focus on Stars.
6. **Mandate beverage upselling** — Train staff to attach beverages to every main course. A 20% beverage attach improvement would add ₹8–10L per quarter.
7. **Launch afternoon Snack + Coffee combos** — Price ₹199–299 bundles for 3–5 PM to fill the idle window. Target delivery channels.
8. **Negotiate Zomato commission aggressively** — At ₹65.5L revenue, even a 1% commission reduction saves ₹65,000+ per quarter.
9. **Replicate Kothrud's top product mix** at all branches — Branches lagging in Rice Bowls & Mains performance are likely missing Kothrud's menu placement/training model.
10. **Build a customer loyalty program** — The transaction data shows repeat purchasing behavior in invoice patterns. A structured loyalty scheme could increase visit frequency by 15–20%.

## Key Assumptions
1. **Sessions:** POS records all sessions as "Unknown." Derived sessions assigned by hour (Breakfast: 7–10, Lunch: 11–14, Snack: 15–17, Dinner: 18–23).
2. **Excluded entities:** Central Kitchen, FC ROAD, Head Office excluded from all branch analysis (no revenue).
3. **Brand filter:** The Hoagie Club brand (₹10K total) excluded from all analysis per instructions.
4. **Net Sales definition:** Gross Sales minus Discounts plus Packaging Charges = Net Sales per transaction.
5. **Food Cost accuracy:** Menu food costs noted as potentially inaccurate by the business owner; used as directional estimates only, not precise margin calculations.
6. **Basket Analysis:** Conducted at category level (not invoice level) due to data structure; item-level combos are approximations.
7. **Forecast:** Linear regression on 3 monthly data points — insufficient for seasonal adjustment; treat as directional only.
8. **Water Bottle SKU:** Items with unit price of ₹0 or ₹9 (water bottle only bills) treated separately from main revenue analysis.
